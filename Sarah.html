Three Cuts Worth Knowing

Tap to flip.

1
Picanha
Tap to flip
The top sirloin cap. The fat stays on, it gets nothing but coarse salt, and it roasts over the fire on a skewer. The Brazilian steakhouse classic.
2
Fraldinha
Tap to flip
Bottom sirloin flap, the same cut used for New England steak tips. Big flavor, but slice it against the grain or it gets chewy.
3
Filet Mignon
Tap to flip
The tenderloin. The most tender cut, but so lean that it’s usually wrapped in bacon to keep it juicy.
